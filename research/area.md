# MVP area: Bengaluru Cantonment core

**Bounding box:** lat 12.962–12.993, lng 77.580–77.622 (about 3.5 × 4.5 km).
Western edge Freedom Park, eastern edge Ulsoor Lake / Holy Trinity, north edge Commercial Street, south edge Bangalore Club / Richmond Town.

## Why here
- **Twin-city history:** the Cantonment was established in 1809 by the British next to Kempe Gowda's 16th-century Pete, with the Parade Ground at its heart. South Parade became MG Road on 26 February 1948.
- **Dense layering at single addresses** (verified in `data/candidates.csv`): the Plaza → metro, Central Jail → Freedom Park, Sampangi Lake → stadium, Victoria Hotel → mall, and Rex (1940s → 2018) stacks.
- **Strongest PD imagery in the city:** ~55 dated images of named landmarks 1839–1923 are all inside the box.
- **Recognition:** nearly every Bengaluru resident has been to MG Road and Brigade Road, which feeds the "wait, THAT was there?" reaction.

## Backups (not scored in depth yet)
- **Pete / Fort / KR Market:** deeper time (Kempe Gowda 1537, Hyder Ali's stone fort 1761, fort mostly demolished), but less PD imagery found so far.
- **Basavanagudi / Malleswaram:** early-1900s planned suburbs; unlikely to have lost-cinema-style layers.

## Known weakness
The box is **uneven**: rich along MG Road and the Cubbon Park edge, thin in residential lanes (Richmond Town, Shivajinagar interiors). The UI must snap to the nearest place instead of showing "nothing here".
