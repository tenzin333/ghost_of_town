## Differentiation thesis

Every competitor answers **"what did this look like?"** with photos or map tiles. None answers **"what *was* this?"**: the chain of uses, names and events at a single spot, such as *furniture shop → Plaza cinema (1936) → Ten Commandments ran 44 weeks → last film 2005 → metro station (2011)*. The differentiator is the **place biography / dig-down stack**: facts first, media second, every layer sourced. A photo is one layer among many.

That also means we don't need a photo for every layer. Much of 1930–2000 (cinemas, hotels, cafés) has no reusable imagery but excellent sourced text, which is exactly where photo-first products are empty.

**Risks:**
- Time Portal could add stories. Our moat is curation quality and local depth, not technology.
- BLR Yesterday owns "old Bengaluru maps". Approach them to partner rather than compete.
