# Licensing & copyright (not legal advice; the rules here are a conservative prototype policy)

## Indian copyright terms (per Commons' India copyright page)
- **Photographs made before 1958**: 50 years from creation, so **every pre-1958 Indian photograph is now PD in India.**
- **Photographs after 1957**: 60 years from publication, so photos published before 1966 are PD in India. The 2012 amendment moved photographs to artistic works (life + 60) for newer works.
- **Anonymous / pseudonymous**: 60 years from first publication.
- **Government works**: 60 years from first publication.
- **Freedom of panorama**: we may use our *own* modern photos of buildings and statues (3D works), but not of murals or paintings.
- **Foreign-published images** (Illustrated London News, missionary magazines, Tuck's postcards, British Library albums): 1839–1923 items are PD in the US and UK as well (authors died 70+ years ago). Commons tags them `PD-old`.

## Prototype rules
1. **Images:** only `Public domain`, `CC0`, `CC BY` or `CC BY-SA` files from Commons, or our own photos. Store `credit`, `licence` and `source_url` per image and show them in the UI.
   - CC BY-SA: display attribution plus licence link. Resized copies stay under the same licence.
2. **Copyrighted material** (news articles, modern press photos, books): **never rehost.** Write our own ≤ 80-word summary of the facts (facts aren't copyrightable) and link the article.
3. **Wikipedia text:** don't copy; summarize and cite. Wikidata is CC0.
4. **Uncertain tags:** a few 1900 Doveton images are tagged CC BY-SA 3.0 even though they're 1900 book scans. Honour the tag as given; it only costs an attribution line.
5. **Maps:** Commons PD plans are fine. BLR Yesterday tiles need **permission** before use.
6. **Basemap:** OSM's tile policy allows low-volume interactive viewing with visible attribution and no SLA. Access "may be withdrawn at any point" for commercial or heavy use, and bulk/offline download is banned. Validation-scale traffic is fine on OSM or Carto tiles; switch to a Protomaps PMTiles file before any launch.
7. **Legends vs facts:** some attractive claims are lore, e.g. "Victoria Hotel began as a reading room built for Winston Churchill", attributed to Maya Jayapal (1997) and reported by The News Minute. Show these as **"Local legend"** with the attribution, never as fact.

Sources: [Commons: Copyright rules — India](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Copyright_rules_by_territory/India), [OSMF tile usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/)
